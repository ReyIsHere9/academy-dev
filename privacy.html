Privacy Policy

Plain-language version first, legal version later.

The short version

  • This demo site stores everything in your own browser (localStorage/sessionStorage). Nothing leaves your machine.
  • Accounts, passwords, payments and photos in this dev build are demo data.
  • The live version will collect only what a real academy needs: your account details, enrollments and payments.

When this goes live (checklist)

  • What we collect: name, email, account activity, payment confirmations (never card numbers — our payment provider handles those).
  • Why: to run your account, deliver classes and lessons, and send the messages you asked for.
  • Where it lives: our hosting provider [provider name], with data processed under a signed agreement.
  • How long: while your account exists, plus [retention period] for billing records.
  • Your rights: access, export, correction and deletion — one email to hello@example-academy.com.
  • Cookies: only what's needed to keep you logged in; analytics only with consent.
